# Code of Conduct

Relook follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be welcoming, patient and respectful, whatever someone's background or experience.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, or publishing anyone's private information.
- Chat exports are personal. Never share someone else's messages, photos or contact details in issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, email the maintainer at **joelercoaster2014@gmail.com**. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, or block people, who don't follow this code.
